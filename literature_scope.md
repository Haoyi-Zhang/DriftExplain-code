# Literature scope and citation discipline

## Positioning target

The article concerns one fixed old/new finite endpoint pair, a canonical block
delta, and exact equality of a framed complete-keyed evidence traversal.  Its
strongest direct baseline is the field-sensitive projection from the final
decoded read closure to the atoms that own those coordinates.  The producer
implements that projection.  The contribution is therefore the representation
theorem establishing when this projection is the unique least explanation,
not a new source dependence analysis, a generic minimal-set algorithm, or a
measured speed advantage.

## Relevance-first comparison set

The final manuscript contains 67 unique scholarly references, all cited in the
text.  The comparison set spans eleven overlapping roles:

1. dependence, slicing, and abstract interpretation (7);
2. data and programming-language provenance (7);
3. structural causality and responsibility (5);
4. debugging, program reduction, and hardness (6);
5. monotone minimal-set theory (3);
6. formal sufficient and abductive explanations (2);
7. change impact, regression, and warning evolution (13);
8. incremental computation and maintenance (7);
9. certificates, validation, and verification witnesses (8);
10. analyzer practice (4); and
11. smart-contract analysis and business-logic interfaces (5).

The total is 67.  Roles are used for organization rather than a claim that the
literatures are disjoint.

## Closest-work correction

The blind audit added the direct monotone-minimal-set and formal-explanation
literature that the earlier draft omitted.  In particular, the manuscript now
credits Minimal Sets over Monotone Predicates, the subsequent query-complexity
and reduction results, abduction-based explanations, and sufficient reasons
behind decisions.  The article explicitly disclaims novelty for generic
co-singleton deletion tests, deletion-core intersection facts, MSMP, and
feature-level sufficient reasons.  Its novelty claim is confined to the
conditions under which a complete analyzer-evidence interface makes its replay
predicate a principal filter and thereby identifies the final-read projection
with the unique least endpoint-edit support.

## Verification surfaces

- `reference_verification.csv` records title, ordered authors, year,
  venue/source, stable identifier, authoritative record, and the exact scope of
  each metadata check.
- `citation_claim_map.csv` maps every citation key to an actual manuscript claim
  and states the intended support role.
- `literature_calibration.csv` records the closest distinction and strongest
  documented limitation for every cited work.
- `external_resources.csv` records stable scholarly or official resources and
  acquisition limits.

These records distinguish bibliographic identity, abstract/focused-passage
inspection, and executed baselines.  No DOI lookup is treated as a substitute
for empirical replication or a claim that every cited paper was reread
cover-to-cover.  The LogicScan entry remains explicitly marked as a preprint.

## Bounded conclusions

The literature supports the following distinctions and no broader priority
claim:

- exact keyed-evidence support is different from value-only sufficient reasons,
  counterfactual causes, and one-minimal failing inputs;
- co-singleton deletion membership is known generic machinery, while the paper's
  representation theorem is what makes it complete for this drift predicate;
- source extraction, identity correspondence, incremental reuse, certificate
  checking, and developer usefulness are separate obligations; and
- the delivered artifact does not discharge source-level semantics, historical
  validity, production performance, or broad detector effectiveness.
