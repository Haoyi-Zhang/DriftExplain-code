# Consumed inputs and source attribution

The exact consumed external-pattern representation is the nine authored records
in `data/public_fixtures.json`. The selection table maps each record to its
retained public source path, annotated line field and anchor. These records are
sufficient to reproduce the finite generators offline. No third-party contract
source is included or executed, and the source-to-fact mapping has not been
independently rederived in this study.

The public repository URLs are mutable browsing references, not immutable source
snapshots. They must not be described as exact reconstruction of contract source.
SmartBugs Curated states that its contracts retain their original licenses;
the repository's other-file license is not blanket permission to redistribute
every contract. The authored fact records are covered by the artifact license,
which expressly does not relicense third-party source.

The four interventions per record are synthetic input changes. They do not
reconstruct historical repairs. A lawful, frozen 120-contract corpus with a
transparent source-extraction contract remains absent.
