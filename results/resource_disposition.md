# Resource disposition of the retained empirical campaign

One full/update pass over the retained timing cases contains 112,846
family-anchor evaluations.  Seven measured pairs account for 789,922 evaluations
and the documented warm-up adds 112,846, giving a lower bound of 902,768 before
cache construction, untimed comparisons, certificate work, mutation work, the
support study, unit tests, or any unretained run.

The declared campaign ceiling was 800,000 analysis or replay obligations.  The
inherited record did not use a consistently defined alternative unit, and prior
cumulative CPU time is unknown.  The discrepancy cannot be repaired by resetting
the allowance, excluding the warm-up after execution, deleting raw records, or
renaming the counting unit.

Accordingly:

- the empirical timing/scaling campaign remains closed as over cap;
- raw timing/scaling files are retained only as exploratory observations and
  negative protocol evidence;
- no runtime, scaling, source-corpus, production-readiness, or completion claim
  relies on those records;
- the current 33-test suite and the three finite checkers are post-disposition
  artifact verification and do not rehabilitate the campaign; and
- no positive resource or performance conclusion is drawn.

This disposition preserves the evidence while preventing retrospective budget
reset or selective deletion.
